* The principles in short

- *A lossless engine.* Every format is parsed into ranges of the file;
  edits replace ranges; nothing is regenerated from a tree.
- *Standard formats stay standard.* Kalem renders and edits Org, LaTeX,
  CSV, BibTeX and code without writing anything their standards do not
  define (Part II).
- *Two frontends, one core.* The graphical editor (gpui) and the
  terminal editor (ratatui) share =kalem-core=: the commands, keymaps,
  settings and view model. A feature is done when it works in both;
  what the terminal cannot show is listed in [[file:terminal-parity.org][Terminal parity]].
- *Emacs as the oracle for Org.* Kalem's Org follows =org-element.el=
  and is tested against it.
- *A small core.* Org, LaTeX, CSV and Markdown are in the core; other
  modes and features come as plugins on the same contracts (Part IV).

- Source view (Ctrl+/) :: the file's own text, with colors, line numbers
  and every character shown.
- Split view (Ctrl+\) :: the source beside the rich view of the same
  document. A click makes a side the active one.
- Outline (Ctrl+Shift+O) :: the headings in a sidebar. A click jumps to a
  heading; dragging one moves its subtree, before the heading it is dropped
  on or, on the lower half, after it.
- Focus mode (F8) :: only the section you are writing in.
- Narrowing :: /Narrow to Subtree/, /Element/ or /Block/ in the command
  palette shows only that part; /Widen/ shows everything again.
- Soft wrap (Alt+Z) :: long lines wrap at the window's edge, or scroll
  sideways.
- Word counts :: the status bar counts the words of the document and of
  the section at the cursor. /Set Document Word Target/ writes
  =#+PROPERTY: WORD_TARGET 80000= and /Set Section Word Target/ a
  =WORD_TARGET= property on the heading (=80,000= and =80k= work too; 0
  removes it); the counts then show against them, "12,400 of 80,000
  (15%) words, 900 of 4,000 (22%) in section". /Word Count by Chapter/
  (View menu) lists the headings of the first two levels with their
  words and targets; choosing one goes there. /Go to Line/ moves to a
  line by its number.

Headings move right by level, and the text under a heading starts where
its title does, as with Emacs's =org-indent-mode=: in the window, two
characters a level from the second on; in the terminal, where a sign
marks each heading, after that sign. =editor.outline_indent = false=, or
=#+STARTUP: noindent= in a file, keeps all text at the left edge (in the
terminal, headings still move right by level).

The text column is 80 characters wide and starts at the left edge of the
window; the setting =editor.line_width= changes its width (0 for the whole
window), and =editor.center_text = true= puts it in the middle, like a
page.

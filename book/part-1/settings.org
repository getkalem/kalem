/Settings/ in the Kalem menu (Ctrl+, or Cmd+,) sets the theme, the
keys, the language, the text size, the font, and whether folders under
version control become projects by themselves. Changes apply to every
window and are saved in =settings.toml= in the settings directory:
=$KALEM_CONFIG_DIR=, or =kalem= in =$XDG_CONFIG_HOME=, =%APPDATA%= on
Windows, or =~/.config=. A project can have its own
=.kalem/settings.toml=, which applies over yours.

In the terminal editor /Settings/ (Ctrl+,, or Alt+, where the terminal
cannot send it; =SPC h v= with Vim's keys; the Kalem menu on F10)
lists every setting, grouped by its table as in =settings.toml=, the
way lazygit lists things: =j= and =k= (or the arrows) choose one, its
default and what it does show below, =h= and =l= (or Space) change it
in place (a switch flipped, the next choice, a number up or down),
Enter asks for a text, =/= filters by key or description, =d= takes a
setting back to its default, =e= opens =settings.toml= (where lists
are edited), and =q= or Escape closes. Values that differ from the
default stand out. A change is saved at once and shows at once; when
the workspace's =.kalem/settings.toml= sets the same key, the status
line says that it wins.

Commands that change a setting, such as /Toggle Folder Tree/ or
/Toggle Line Numbers/, save it too. After editing a settings file or
=keymap.json= by hand, /Reload Settings and Keys/ (=help.reload=, in
the palette; =SPC h r r= with Vim's keys) reads them again in both
editors.

* Themes

Light and dark themes follow the system unless =editor.theme= says
otherwise; the terminal editor asks the terminal for its background.
To change colors, write =themes/light.toml= or =themes/dark.toml= in
the settings directory with a =[colors]= table of the colors to
change, as =#rrggbb= or =#rrggbbaa=; the others keep their values:

#+begin_src toml
[colors]
background = "#fdf6e3"
link = "#268bd2"
#+end_src

The built-in themes, with every color's name and what it colors, are
=crates/kalem-core/themes/light.toml= and =dark.toml= in Kalem's
source.

/Settings/ in the Kalem menu (Ctrl+, or Cmd+,; in the terminal Alt+,
where the terminal cannot send Ctrl+,; =SPC h v= with Vim's keys)
lists every setting, in both editors the same way. Changes apply to
every window and are saved in =settings.toml= in the settings
directory: =$KALEM_CONFIG_DIR=, or =kalem= in =$XDG_CONFIG_HOME=,
=%APPDATA%= on Windows, or =~/.config=. A project can have its own
=.kalem/settings.toml=, which applies over yours.

The settings are grouped by their table as in =settings.toml=, the way
lazygit lists things: =j= and =k= (or the arrows) choose one, its
default and what it does show below, =h= and =l= (or the left and right
arrows, or Space) change it in place: a switch flipped, the next
choice, a number up or down, and for a text the next of its usual
values, so that little is typed: the fonts installed for the fonts,
the TeX engines for =latex.engine=, search sites for
=search.online_url=, =LOGBOOK= for =org.log_into_drawer=… Enter types
a value of your own (in the graphical editor in place, the usual values
holding what is typed offered below it: Down chooses one, Tab
completes), =/= filters by key or description, =d= takes a setting
back to its default, =e= opens =settings.toml=, Escape goes back (or
closes) and =q= closes. A click chooses a setting and a click on the
chosen one changes it. Values that differ from the default stand out.
A change is saved at once and shows at once; when the workspace's
=.kalem/settings.toml= sets the same key, the status line says that it
wins.

Enter on a list or a table shows its items in the same panel, so that
no setting needs the file: in a list of choices (=editor.vim.modes=)
Space puts a choice in or out; in a list of texts (=org.todo_keywords=,
=plugins.sources=) Enter edits an item, =a= adds one, =x= removes it,
and =J= and =K= move it down and up; in =files.modes= an entry is typed
as =path = mode= and =h= and =l= step its mode. Escape goes back to the
settings.

/installed plugins/, under =plugins=, lists the plugins installed; Enter
on one shows its page: a language plugin's server (=auto=, one of those
it offers, or =off=), the settings its manifest describes (see
[[file:../part-3/plugins.org][Plugins]]), changed the same way, any other key of its
=[plugins."ID"]= table (typed as JSON), then updating it, its folder
and removing it. Escape goes back a page at a time.

Commands that change a setting, such as /Toggle Folder Tree/ or
/Toggle Line Numbers/, save it too. After editing a settings file or
=keymap.json= by hand, /Reload Settings and Keys/ (=help.reload=, in
the palette; =SPC h r r= with Vim's keys) reads them again in both
editors.

* Themes

Light and dark themes follow the system unless =editor.theme= says
otherwise; the terminal editor asks the terminal for its background.
To change colors, write =themes/light.toml= or =themes/dark.toml= in
the settings directory with a =[colors]= table of the colors to
change, as =#rrggbb= or =#rrggbbaa=; the others keep their values:

#+begin_src toml
[colors]
background = "#fdf6e3"
link = "#268bd2"
#+end_src

The built-in themes, with every color's name and what it colors, are
=crates/kalem-core/themes/light.toml= and =dark.toml= in Kalem's
source.

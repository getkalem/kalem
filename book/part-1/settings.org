Choose /Settings/ in the Kalem menu (Ctrl+, or Cmd+,) for the theme, the
keys, the language, the text size and the font. Changes apply to every
window and are saved in =settings.toml=, in the settings directory:
=$KALEM_CONFIG_DIR=, or =kalem= in =$XDG_CONFIG_HOME=, =%APPDATA%= on
Windows, or =~/.config=. A project can have its own
=.kalem/settings.toml=, which applies over yours.

The terminal editor has no settings panel: /Settings/ (Ctrl+,, or Alt+,
where the terminal cannot send it) shows the path of =settings.toml= in
the status line. Open the file and edit it there, or use the commands
that change a setting, such as /Toggle Folder Tree/.

Kalem reads its settings files when it starts. After editing one by
hand, in either editor, restart Kalem; a change made in the settings
panel or by such a command reads your =settings.toml= again at once.

* Themes

Light and dark themes follow the system unless =editor.theme= says
otherwise; the terminal editor asks the terminal for its background. To
change colors, write =themes/light.toml= or =themes/dark.toml= in the
settings directory with a =[colors]= table holding the colors to change,
as =#rrggbb= or =#rrggbbaa=; colors you leave out keep their values:

#+begin_src toml
[colors]
background = "#fdf6e3"
link = "#268bd2"
#+end_src

The built-in themes, with every color's name and a comment on what it
colors, are =crates/kalem-core/themes/light.toml= and =dark.toml= in
Kalem's source.

Kalem opens any text file. Org, LaTeX, CSV and BibTeX files get the views
of their formats ([[file:writing-in-org.org][Writing in Org]], [[file:latex-files.org][LaTeX files]], which covers =.bib=
files too, and [[file:csv-files.org][CSV files]]); Markdown, code, configuration files and every other text
file are edited as plain text: monospace, colored by their language, with line
numbers, the cursor's line marked and indentation guides. Tab follows the
file's indentation (tabs, or its number of spaces) and indents selected
lines; Shift+Tab outdents.

In code, Enter keeps the line's indentation and goes a level deeper
after an opening bracket (after =:= in Python and YAML); between a pair,
={|}=, the closing bracket moves to a line of its own. A closing bracket
typed alone on its line goes back a level. The bracket matching the one
at the cursor is marked, and /Go to Matching Bracket/ (Ctrl+Alt+B) goes
to it. /Toggle Comment/ (Ctrl+Alt+C) comments out the selected lines with
the language's marker (=//=, =#=, =--=, =;=, =%=, or =<!-- … -->= in HTML
and Markdown), or uncomments them; in an Org document it works in source
blocks with their language, and elsewhere writes Org's =#= comments.

/Duplicate Lines/ (Ctrl+Shift+D), /Move Lines Up/ and /Down/ (Alt+Up and
Alt+Down), /Join Lines/, /Sort Lines/ and /Trim Trailing Whitespace/ act
on the lines of the selection (the keys are those of plain text; Org
keeps Alt+Up and Down for headings, items and rows). /Go to Line/ asks
for a line number. /Expand Selection/ (Ctrl+Alt+Right) grows the
selection to the word, the inside of the brackets around it and the
brackets, the line and the paragraph (in Org documents, the object and
the element around it), and /Shrink Selection/ (Ctrl+Alt+Left) goes back.

The kind of a file comes from, in order: your choice with the command
/Set Document Mode/ (remembered in the workspace settings), a mode line
such as =-*- mode: org -*-= on the first line, the extension, and a
=#!= line. Markdown files are edited as text for now. Binary files are
not opened.

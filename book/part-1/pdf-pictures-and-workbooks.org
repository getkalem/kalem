PDF files, pictures and Excel workbooks open in viewers: plugins that
run in Kalem's sandbox, built into it (see [[file:../part-3/plugins.org][Plugins]]). A file that is
text, an SVG picture among them, opens as text; =kalem view FILE= shows
a viewer's file on the command line.

The keys are a viewer's own while it has the focus: Page Down and Page
Up, or =n= and =p=, go to the next and the previous page or frame, Home
and End to the first and the last; =+= and =-= zoom, =0= fits the
window, =w= the width and =1= shows the actual size; the arrows (or
=h= =j= =k= =l=) pan; =r= turns the view; =i= shows the file's
information; =y= copies the picture; Shift+N and Shift+P open the next
and the previous file of the folder. Find (Ctrl+F) searches a PDF's
text. The terminal editor draws a page or a picture through its
terminal's graphics (kitty's, iTerm2's or Sixel), else in half-block
cells, and with colors off shows its text.

- PDF :: Pages with their outline in the sidebar, their page labels and
  their links: one to a page turns to it, and one that leaves the
  document (a web address, a file, another program) asks first and
  shows where it goes. Text is selected and copied as on paper. A PDF
  that LaTeX built goes back to its source line on Ctrl-click (Cmd-click
  on macOS). A PDF with a password asks for it, hidden as typed, and
  keeps it until Kalem quits.
- Pictures :: PNG and APNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, BMP, TIFF, ICO, QOI, TGA,
  the PNM family, DDS, OpenEXR and Radiance HDR. An animation plays
  (=.= pauses it); a photograph is turned as its EXIF orientation says;
  the information names the camera, the lens and the exposure. A
  picture larger than 8,192 pixels on its longer side, or 40 megapixels
  over all its frames, is shown scaled down, and the information gives
  both sizes.
- Workbooks :: =.xlsx=, =.xlsm=, =.xltx= and =.xltm= open as their sheets,
  as grids with their styles, formulas and charts, and are edited as a
  spreadsheet is: typing in a cell, formulas recalculated, rows and
  columns inserted and deleted with every reference moved, sorting,
  filters, number formats, charts, and macros run when you ask for one
  by name. The menus have its commands where Excel has them, and a
  right click on a cell offers what is there (a note's, a link's, a
  table's, a pivot table's items); the status bar sums the cells
  selected: their Average, Count and Sum, then their Min, Max and, when
  some are not numbers, the Numerical Count. Saving writes only what you
  changed, every other part of the file copied byte for byte, and asks
  Excel to recalculate the formulas when it opens the file. An =.ods=, =.xls= or =.xlsb= is
  converted as it opens: Kalem asks before saving it back in its own
  format, saying what it keeps (number formats are lost), and Save As
  =.xlsx= leaves the original alone. A workbook with a password to open
  asks for it, and is saved encrypted again with it (saved as =.ods=, it
  is not encrypted, and Kalem says so).

Word documents (=.docx=, =.docm=, =.dotx=, =.dotm=) open through the
=docx= plugin, installed from the plugin list: as a document of the
editor, each paragraph a line with its style, typed into as text, its
comments and tracked changes reviewed with the commands of the /Review/
category. [[file:../part-3/word-documents.org][Word documents]] has the details.

A viewer that fails, runs past its time or out of its memory stops: the
editor closes its document at once and says why, and goes on. A viewer
that stops three times is turned off until it is updated, and a newer
copy installed opens its files in its place, even one installed while
Kalem runs: no restart is needed. =kalem plugin enable ID= turns it on
again.

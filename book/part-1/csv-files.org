CSV and TSV files open as a grid: each field padded to its column's
width, =│= between the fields and the header row bold. The grid is
drawn over the text, so each field is edited where it stands, and the
delimiter (=,=, =;=, a tab or =|=), the quoting, the line endings and a
byte order mark stay as the file has them. A spreadsheet in a Turkish
or other European locale writes =;= between fields and =,= in numbers;
both are read.

Tab and Shift+Tab go from field to field (Tab past the last field adds
a row). The commands of the /CSV/ category insert, delete and move
rows and columns, and /Sort File by Column/ sorts the records by the
column at the cursor (numbers as numbers, the header first); every
change is one undo step, and only the fields that change are written,
quoted only when they need it. /Copy as Tab-Separated Values/ copies
the selected records (all of them without a selection) as a
spreadsheet pastes them, and rows pasted from a spreadsheet get the
file's delimiter. The status bar shows the count, sum, average,
smallest and largest of the numbers in the cursor's column, and the
header row stays at the top when the rows scroll.

In the /Table/ menu: /Filter Rows/ asks for a text and shows only the
rows with a field holding it (case ignored), with the header and the
row at the cursor, and says how many match; /Show All Rows/ shows them
all again. /Sort View by Column/ shows the rows in the order of the
column at the cursor (again: descending) and /File Order/ ends it;
neither changes the file. /Open as Plain Text/ shows the text as it
is, and /Convert to Org Table/ writes the rows as an Org table in a
new =.org= file beside it and opens it.

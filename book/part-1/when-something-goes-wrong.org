Kalem keeps a log, =kalem.log=, in its state directory: =$KALEM_STATE_DIR=
when it is set, else =kalem= in =$XDG_STATE_HOME=, in =%LOCALAPPDATA%= on
Windows, or in =~/.local/state=. Each start begins a new log and keeps
the two before it as =kalem.log.1= and =kalem.log.2=, so the log of a
session that went wrong is still there after one restart.

How much goes into the log is the setting =log.level= (=error=, =warn=,
=info=, the default, =debug= or =trace=). The environment variable
=KALEM_LOG= overrides it for one run, and can name a level per part of
Kalem: =KALEM_LOG=debug kalem notes.org=, or
=KALEM_LOG=kalem_core=debug,info= for more detail from the core only.

When Kalem crashes, it writes the error with its backtrace into the log
and a report beside it, =crash-DATE.txt=: the version, the platform, the
settings files and their problems, and the error. Please attach the
report and the log to a bug report, with the output of =kalem tui
--detect= for problems in the terminal.

Kalem keeps a log, =kalem.log=, in its state directory:
=$KALEM_STATE_DIR= when it is set, else =kalem= in =$XDG_STATE_HOME=,
in =%LOCALAPPDATA%= on Windows, or in =~/.local/state=. Each start
begins a new log and keeps the two before it as =kalem.log.1= and
=kalem.log.2=, so the log of a session that went wrong is still there
after a restart.

The setting =log.level= (=error=, =warn=, =info=, the default,
=debug= or =trace=) says how much goes into the log. The environment
variable =KALEM_LOG= overrides it for one run, per part of Kalem too:
=KALEM_LOG=debug kalem notes.org=, or =KALEM_LOG=kalem_core=debug,info=
for more detail from the core only.

When Kalem crashes, it writes the error with its backtrace into the
log and a report beside it, =crash-DATE.txt=: the version, the
platform, the settings files and their problems, and the error. Please
attach the report and the log to a bug report, with the output of
=kalem tui --detect= for problems in the terminal.

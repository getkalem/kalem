Ctrl+F finds, Ctrl+H finds and replaces. Every match is marked; Enter and
Shift+Enter step through them. In the replace field, Enter replaces one
match and Alt+Enter all of them, in one undo step. Alt+R, or the =.*=
button, makes the search a regular expression; the replacement can use
=$1= for its groups.

Ctrl+Shift+P (or F1) opens the command palette: every command that
applies, by its title, with its category and keys. Type part of a title
to find it; a command's ID (=org.todo.cycle=, or =org todo cycle=)
finds it too, and =kalem commands= lists the IDs. Commands that need
something, such as a heading level, ask for it. What you type there, as
in any question Kalem asks, is edited as a line: the left and right
arrows, Home, End, Backspace and Delete, by words with Ctrl (Option on
macOS). In a terminal the palette is Ctrl+G, and the status line says so.

/Search Lines/ (=SPC s b= with Vim keys, and =SPC s s=) lists the lines
of the document that hold every word typed, in any order, and the
cursor goes to the chosen one as you move through the list: Enter stays
there, Escape goes back. Lower-case words match either case; a word
with a capital matches exactly. =SPC s B= searches every open document,
=SPC s i= lists the headings (=SPC s I= of every open document), and
=SPC s S= starts with the word at the cursor. =SPC s d= searches the
files under the document's folder, =SPC s D= another folder, =SPC s P=
another project, and =SPC s o= looks the word or the selection up
online (the setting =search.online_url= says where).

The menus hold the commands of the document you are in: a LaTeX file
has LaTeX's, a CSV file a Table menu. On macOS they are in the menu bar
at the top of the screen; on Linux and Windows the window has its own
menu bar above the toolbar (the setting =ui.menu_bar = false= hides
it). F10 lists every menu item in the palette, titled with its menu
(/Edit › Select All/), in both editors: it is how the terminal editor,
which has no menu bar, reaches them. Where this book names a menu item,
F10 and its name find it.

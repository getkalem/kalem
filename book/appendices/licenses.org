* Components

Kalem embeds the KaTeX fonts for formulas (SIL Open Font License 1.1),
and uses the Rust crates
listed in =Cargo.lock=, each under its own license.

The highlighter embeds Sublime Text syntax definitions through the
=two-face= crate (MIT or Apache-2.0): the default set that the =syntect=
crate (MIT) ships, from Sublime Text's =Packages= repository under its
MIT-style license, and the further syntaxes of =bat=, each under the
license its source repository gives: MIT for most, Apache-2.0 or a BSD
license for some. =bat='s =assets/syntaxes= and =two-face='s
acknowledgements (its =acknowledgement= module) list each one with its
license.

The citation styles Kalem ships for =#+CITE_EXPORT: csl= (=apa=, =ieee=,
=chicago-author-date= and the others) and their locales are Citation
Style Language styles from the CSL project, under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 license (CC BY-SA 3.0). They come embedded in
the =hayagriva= crate (MIT or Apache-2.0), whose archive records each
style's authors.

The table of Org's entities (=\alpha=, =\nbsp=: 414 names with their
LaTeX, HTML, ASCII, Latin-1 and UTF-8 renderings), in
=crates/org-syntax/src/tables/entities.rs=, is generated from
Emacs's =org-entities.el=, which is part of GNU Emacs under the GPL,
version 3 or later. Whether a permissively licensed crate may carry it
is decision D18, open; see
[[file:../part-5/decisions/D18-entity-table-provenance.org][D18: Entity
table provenance]]. Until it is decided, =org-syntax= is not published
on crates.io.

* Test corpus

The files of =tests/corpus= come from other projects under their own
licenses: the Org manual (GNU FDL 1.3 or later), Org's example files
and ORG-NEWS (GPL 3 or later), Worg's Org Syntax (GNU FDL 1.3 or later),
and files written for Kalem (MIT or Apache-2.0). =tests/corpus/LICENSES.md= records each source,
its revision and its license.

* Components

Kalem embeds the KaTeX fonts for formulas (SIL Open Font License 1.1),
and uses the Rust crates
listed in =Cargo.lock=, each under its own license.

The highlighter embeds Sublime Text syntax definitions: the default set
that the =syntect= crate ships (from Sublime Text's =Packages=
repository and others, each under its own license, mostly MIT-style),
and the further syntaxes of =bat= that the =two-face= crate ships, each
under the license its source repository gives; =bat='s
=assets/syntaxes= and its acknowledgements file list them.

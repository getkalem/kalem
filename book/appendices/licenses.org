* Test corpus

The files of =tests/corpus= come from other projects under their own
licenses: the Org manual (GNU FDL 1.3 or later), Org's example files
and ORG-NEWS (GPL 3 or later), Worg's Org Syntax (GNU FDL 1.3 or later),
the READMEs of eleven projects (MIT, Apache-2.0 or the Unlicense, as
each project is), Foam's documentation (MIT), formulas of the Open
Logic Project (CC BY 4.0) and the HoTT book (CC BY-SA 3.0), arXiv
papers' LaTeX sources (CC BY 4.0), and files written for Kalem (MIT or
Apache-2.0). =tests/corpus/LICENSES.md= records each source, its
revision and its license. The corpus is for the tests; none of it is in
Kalem's binaries.

* Components

=THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md=, beside the program in every release archive
and made by =tools/third-party-licenses.sh=, gives the license of
everything the binaries are built from or embed, with its text: the
Rust crates of =Cargo.lock= and of the plugins built in, among them
gpui (Zed's, Apache-2.0), Wasmtime (Apache-2.0 with the LLVM
exception), IronCalc (MIT or Apache-2.0) and comrak (BSD-2-Clause),
through Kalem's forks =getkalem/ironcalc= and =getkalem/comrak=, and
hayro (MIT or Apache-2.0); and the works that are not code. Of these:

- the KaTeX fonts for formulas (SIL Open Font License 1.1);
- PDFium's Foxit fonts for PDF's standard fonts (PDFium's BSD-style
  license), two colour profiles (one CC0 1.0, one generated with Little
  CMS) and Adobe's character maps (Adobe's BSD-style license), embedded
  by hayro for PDF files and LaTeX's PDF figures;
- Vim's table of digraphs, as =:digraphs= lists it, for the Vim keys
  (Vim's license).

The highlighter embeds Sublime Text syntax definitions through the
=two-face= crate (MIT or Apache-2.0): the default set that the =syntect=
crate (MIT) ships, from Sublime Text's =Packages= repository under its
MIT-style license, and the further syntaxes of =bat=, each under the
license its source repository gives: MIT for most, Apache-2.0 or a BSD
license for some. =bat='s =assets/syntaxes= and =two-face='s
acknowledgements (its =acknowledgement= module) list each one with its
license.

The citation styles Kalem ships for =#+CITE_EXPORT: csl= (=apa=, =ieee=,
=chicago-author-date= and the others) and their locales are Citation
Style Language styles from the CSL project, under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 license (CC BY-SA 3.0). They come embedded in
the =hayagriva= crate (MIT or Apache-2.0), whose archive records each
style's authors.

The table of Org's entities (=\alpha=, =\nbsp=: 414 names with their
LaTeX, HTML, ASCII, Latin-1 and UTF-8 renderings), in
=crates/org-syntax/src/tables/entities.rs=, is generated from
Emacs's =org-entities.el=, which is part of GNU Emacs under the GPL,
version 3 or later. Whether a permissively licensed crate may carry it
is decision D18, open; see
[[file:../part-5/decisions/D18-entity-table-provenance.org][D18: Entity
table provenance]]. Until it is decided, =org-syntax= is not published
on crates.io.

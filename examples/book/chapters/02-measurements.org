[[tab:timings]] lists how long each export of this sample took, and the
total is computed by the table itself. The energy of a body at rest,
equation [[eq:energy]], is the usual example of a displayed
formula.[fn:formula]

Inline mathematics works too: the mean of $n$ runs is
$\bar{x} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i$. Back in
[[#sec:plain-text][the first chapter]], the pipeline of
[[#sec:pipeline]] produced these files.

[fn:formula] See [cite:@knuth84 p. 168] for how TeX sets displayed
equations.
